# Bozze email Le Fonti Awards® (2 novembre 2026)

Base: l'email generica in uso oggi. Campi MailUp tra parentesi quadre: `[nome]`, `[azienda]`, `[specializzazione]`, `[citta]` (solo Avvocati), `[apertura1]`, `[apertura2]` (Asia PMI e Startup).
`[apertura1] [apertura2]` = due frasi su misura (max 100 caratteri l'una), scritte dall'AI solo con fatti del sito ufficiale (fase C, non ancora attiva). Se il sito non basta, l'AI mette una frase standard di riserva: il testo resta sempre corretto.

**Da confermare (segnato con ⚠️):** esistenza di una categoria dedicata ai penalisti / alle startup.

## 1. Avvocati (classica, con la specializzazione)

**Oggetto:** [nome], Le Fonti Awards® 2026: recognising excellence in [specializzazione]
*(alternativa: Le Fonti Awards® 2026: applications are open for criminal law firms)*

Dear [nome],

Applications are officially open for the Le Fonti Awards®, a prestigious international award celebrating excellence in business and in the legal profession. We are reaching out because of [azienda]'s work in [specializzazione] in [citta].

For over 15 years, Le Fonti Awards® have recognised outstanding law firms and practitioners alongside leading companies and professionals, through events held in major business hubs around the world, including Milan, New York, London, Dubai, Singapore and Hong Kong. ⚠️ This year's edition also highlights excellence in criminal law, including [specializzazione].

Beyond the recognition, Le Fonti Awards® open the door to an International Business Community of more than 10.5 million people, including a dedicated community of criminal lawyers, where new connections, collaborations and ideas can take shape.

If you would like to apply, you can complete the online application form: www.lefontiawards.com/application/

For details about the selection criteria and regulations, please visit: www.lefontiawards.com/selection/

Should you require further information, simply reply to this email and a member of our team will get back to you personally.

> `[specializzazione]` = area del penale in cui lo studio lavora, scelta dall'AI dalle aree di attività del sito, scritta in inglese. Esempi: *white-collar crime* · *tax crime* · *financial crime and anti-money laundering* · *cybercrime* · *environmental and workplace safety offences* · *medical liability* · *drug offences* · *road traffic offences* · *extradition and international criminal law*. Se il sito non specifica: *criminal defence*.
